# Action plan — v4.9.0 opens on the mutation debt it inherited

> **Why this plan exists, and why it opens on this.** The v4.8.0 mutation pass measured **16 production
> files**. Thirteen ended at 94 % or above, one at 100 % — and **three did not**, all three for the same
> two structural reasons. The owner **arbitrated them into v4.9.0** on 2026-08-05, with the risk stated
> out loud: it is the **same locally-right deferral** as v4.5.0 and v4.6.0, and nothing ever brought
> those back. What must carry it this time is **not memory** — hence this file, opened the day the
> deferral was taken rather than the day someone remembers it.
>
> **The evidence is already written and must not be re-derived**: `maintainers/mutation/RESULTS.md`
> § v4.8.0 (the table, the two numbered debts, the equivalence lists, the measurement anomaly), and the
> archived plan `archived/field-findings-2026-08-02-action.md` § Step 14.8 (the batch-by-batch record and
> the owner's arbitration in his own words).
>
> **This is a floor, not a ceiling.** v4.9.0's own scope is the owner's to decide; these two items are
> what the previous release **owes**, and they are the head of it.

## Tracking

- [ ] **Debt 1 — a shared `runAsEntrypoint(meta, argv, fn)`, tested once, plus the guard test that makes
      it stick.**
  - [ ] Every top-level `scripts/*.mjs` ends with an `isEntrypoint(import.meta.url, process.argv[1])`
        block **no test can import**. Files whose whole body is that shape score **0 %**:
        `upstream-check-run.mjs` (new in v4.8.0 — it fell into the shape the same week the shape was
        named) and `session-status.mjs` (**inherited**, named as debt since v4.4.0 and carried by every
        published tag since). `status-line.mjs` is the third known one.
  - [ ] **20 files carry the shape.** Measured at v4.8.0, not estimated — re-count before starting, then
        write the number down here.
  - [ ] The design was settled at v4.5.0 and is not to be re-opened: **one** shared helper, tested once,
        with the per-script body passed in as a function.
  - [ ] **The guard test is the half that makes it stick** (without it, the next new script re-creates
        the debt): a harness-suite test that goes **red** when a top-level `scripts/*.mjs` has no
        `.test.mjs` sibling, **and** when a module builds and executes a child process in the same
        function. It carries an **allowlist that may only SHRINK** — a list that can grow is a list that
        will.
  - [ ] Re-measure the touched files afterwards and record the numbers in `RESULTS.md` (a
        hardened-but-unmeasured file has an unknown score).
- [ ] **Debt 2 — `defaultGit` turned into a pure value.**
  - [ ] `scripts/lib/engine-fetch.mjs:104-109` is the real git runner and **21 mutants live in it**
        (the file scores **54.05 %**, its lowest-scoring neighbour by a wide margin).
  - [ ] **The identical shape was solved once already**, at v4.5.0 in `verify-index.mjs`: the spawn's
        request became a **pure value** (`buildCrosscheckInvocation` → `{command, args, options}`,
        asserted whole, `win32` fed on purpose), which killed 19 survivors. It was **never propagated**.
        Mirror it rather than invent a second shape.
  - [ ] The code currently **documents its own exemption** — *"Used by the core's CLI wiring, never by
        the unit tests"* — which is exactly what `tdd-discipline` rule 6 forbids (*« pure glue, pas
        testable » n'est jamais une excuse, c'est le diagnostic*). Delete that comment with the fix.
  - [ ] Re-measure `engine-fetch.mjs` and record the number.
- [ ] **Explicitly NOT in scope, and not to be re-proposed** _(owner, 2026-08-05)_: an **11th written
      reflex** in `tdd-discipline`. That is what the previous month already did, and the v4.8.0 pass is
      what measured its insufficiency. The adopted counter-measure is a **process** one and already
      shipped: `CONVENTIONS.md` **§5quinquies** — a new production file is mutated the **day it is
      written**, not at the release tail.
- [ ] **When this lands, close the loop**: `RESULTS.md` § v4.8.0's two numbered debts each get a line
      saying which release paid them, so the debt list cannot quietly outlive its own repayment.
