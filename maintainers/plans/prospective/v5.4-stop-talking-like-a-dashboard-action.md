## Why this release has an end, and what the end is

**The parent plan's own reservation was that this half is "weeks with no announceable end".** That is
true of *"rewrite every string"* and false of the work below, and the difference is the unit:

- **A string is not a unit of work** — there are 3 675 lines of delivered skills and 1 361 lines of
  constitution, and no one can say when they have been "audited".
- **A gesture is.** A gesture is something the brain *does to a human*: it asks, it announces, or it
  acts. They live in a finite, listable set of places, and each one is either right as it stands or
  it changes tier. **Step 1 produces that list**, and the day it exists this release has a bottom.

🎯 **And the sentence on the tin already exists**, which is what the parent plan doubted: *"your brain
stops interrupting you to ask things you would never have wanted to be asked, and says the rest in
words you can read."*

🛑 **The single most important distinction in this whole release, and it is easy to get wrong.**
A `SKILL.md` is mostly **instructions to the model**, not text a human reads. The jargon count across
the delivered prose is an **upper bound, not a to-do list**: `frontmatter` 32, `fan-out` 16,
`dangling` 13, `reconcile` 11, `chunk` 8, `orphan` 7, `idempotent` 5. Most of those are legitimate —
the model needs the precise word to do the right thing. **What this release changes is only what the
brain is told to SAY, and what the engine PRINTS.** An audit that "fixes" the instructions instead
would make the brain vaguer and no plainer.

## Step 1 — the register: every gesture the brain makes, and its tier

makes steps 2–6 estimable instead of open-ended.

      owner. The perimeter is the table above; the unit is the gesture, never the file.
- [x] **One row per gesture:** where it lives · what it does today (asks / announces / acts silently) ·
      **its tier under ADR 0043** · **verdict: keep, or change, and to what**.
- [x] **Classify with the ADR's own gate**, in its order: *reversibility × confidence*. The ADR's

- [x] **Count the verdicts and put the number in this plan's STATE.** **62 gestures · 13 change
      (10 tier, 3 wording) · 3 wait on him · 46 keep.**
- [x] 🎯 **And the count says something the plan did not expect: the defect is CONCENTRATED.**
      **Ten of the thirteen changes live in two places** — the startup screen and `/lint` — which are

> 📐 **Why a register and not a checklist inside this plan.** The list outlives v5.4: every new skill
> adds a gesture, and the question *"which tier is this?"* is asked again each time. A plan is
> archived when it ships; a register is read by the next one. It is also what a later guard would
> read, if step 6's idea is ever widened.

### The one question step 1 will raise, and it is his

**Some gestures will stop asking.** That is the entire point of the 🟢 tier and it is exactly the
change an owner could experience as *"it did something without me"*. The ADR already answers it in
principle — auto-commit makes every 🟢 one `git revert` away — but the **list of what specifically
goes silent** is a product call, not a design one. So: the register is written first, and the 🟢 rows
are put to him **as one list, once**, rather than gesture by gesture as they are implemented.

## Step 2 — the startup screen stops showing its own stage directions

**This is the highest-frequency surface in the product: the owner meets it before typing a word, in
every session.** It is also the one with a verbatim field capture, in
[`studies/fleet-upgrade-field-feedback.md` § F5](../../studies/fleet-upgrade-field-feedback.md).

- [x] 🛑 **The channel fact is ESTABLISHED, and it INVERTS the July diagnosis** _(2026-09-12, Claude
      Code v2.1.220, macOS · the matrix, with how each cell was measured, is in
      [`registers/gestures.md`](../../registers/gestures.md) § *The channel matrix, re-measured*)_.
      **`systemMessage` is the OWNER's channel** — the CLI prints it, prefixed
      `SessionStart:<matcher> says:` on its first line — and **`additionalContext` is the AGENT's**,
      never printed. Established two ways: the host's own renderer produces `says:` for exactly one
      attachment type, `hook_system_message`; and a probe (a throwaway folder, a hook emitting a
      distinct word on each channel, one headless question) brought back **only the
      `additionalContext` word**.
  - [x] **So the leak was never an echo — it is OUR OWN prose in the owner's channel.** The July
        note said `session-universe.mjs` *"emits no `systemMessage` at all"*; it does not build the
        payload, `buildUniverseHookOutput` does, and at that very commit it emitted
        `systemMessage: [nudge, digest, offer].join("\n")`. The unprefixed line that looked like a
        second channel is the second line of one multi-line `systemMessage`.
  - [x] ⚠️ **And the wrong reason has a live cost, which is why this is worth the paragraph.** The
        v4.4.0 fix was right under either diagnosis, so it worked and nobody re-opened it — while
        **four emitters kept routing directives into `systemMessage` afterwards**, because the
        diagnosis said that channel was the safe one. Those four are this step's work.

**What is still true today** *(verified 2026-09-12, not assumed)*: the payloads are far shorter than
the July capture — progressive disclosure landed — and **four of them still put agent-directed prose
in front of the owner**, because they route it through `systemMessage`:

- `universe-reminder.mjs` — the **working-context synthesis**, whose own directive says *"use it
  silently, do not recite it"*, is recited to the CLI owner verbatim; and the **profile offer**, which
  reaches them as *"No profile yet for this owner's context. Offer once, in their language …
  (never use the word `universe`: they have never met the notion)"* — the instruction instead of the
  offer, vocabulary rule included.
- `brain-author.mjs` — all **three** of its messages, each carrying a shell command with flags:
  *"ASK before today's first note … NEVER guess. Record their answer: `node
  scripts/author-identity.mjs --same-person "<name>"`"*.
- [x] **Separate the two readers in the payload itself**, rather than writing one string that must
      serve both: the agent needs the routing (`offer /lint`), the owner needs the fact. Today one
      string carries both, which is why the echo leaks. _(2026-09-12 — the routing left
      `systemMessage` in all four emitters; `brain-author.mjs` now emits **no** owner channel at all,
      because all three of its messages were directions to the agent and none was a fact.)_
- [x] **Exact-message tests on every emitter** — `wiki-health-nudge.mjs`, `universe-reminder.mjs`,
      `brain-author.mjs`, `engine-divergence-nudge.mjs`, `actions-log-seed.mjs`,
      `status-hook-output.mjs` — asserting what a **human** sees, not that a substring survives.
      _(2026-09-12 — all six carry `directiveTraces(...) === []`, the mechanical half of the wording
      rule, in `scripts/lib/owner-facing.mjs`.)_
- [x] 🎯 **The shipped invariant is NARROW, and deliberately so: a string the owner reads carries no
      instruction to the model.** What it does **not** say is that the owner should read less. The
      facts the CLI shows today (the wiki counts, the working-context nudge, the engine-version line)
      are **duplicated** by the agent's relay, and deleting them would be a product change — the owner
      would stop seeing something he sees today. That is his call, not a session's, so they stay.
- [x] ⚠️ **Do not mute the agent's relay to fix the echo.** F5 names this trap by hand: the relayed
      version is the good one, and the owner currently meets the same offer twice.

## Step 3 — one batched announcement replaces the cascade (the 🟡 tier, built once)

ADR 0043 §4 calls this *"the whole cure"*, and **it does not exist yet**: there is no shared way for
the brain to say *"here are the six things I am about to do"* and act unless vetoed. Every skill that
needs it would otherwise invent its own, and five inventions of one rule is the drift the ADR warns
about.

- [x] **Build it once, in the engine**, deterministically (ADR 0009): the core composes the batch and
      the skill relays it, the way `nativeConnectorsReminder` and `conversationResidueReminder`
      already work in `universes.mjs`. _(2026-09-12 — `scripts/lib/batched-announcement.mjs`,
      `planBatch` + `batchMessage`, 12 tests.)_
- [x] **The owner's move is a VETO, not an authorisation** — silence proceeds. Assert that asymmetry
      in a test, in both directions: nothing said ⇒ it ran; *stop* ⇒ nothing ran. _(Pinned word for
      word, plus a test that the message contains no question mark and no "shall I".)_
- [x] **A count, then the list** — the same progressive disclosure his own words asked for in July:
      the trigger first, the detail only if wanted.
- [x] 🛑 **A gesture that escapes auto-commit may not be 🟡.** The net is what buys the tier (ADR 0043
      §3). Before a batch may act, assert that what it writes lands in a commit. _(`planBatch` splits
      on `committed`, and an uncommitted gesture is handed back as something to **ask**, never
      silently dropped — dropping it would lose the gesture entirely.)_

## Step 4 — `/lint`, gesture by gesture

**The skill that produced the complaint**, and the most concrete instance of every defect in #79.
Its five report categories are named in the machinery's vocabulary, and **all five fixes are 🔴 today**
because the skill was built on the old binary posture: *propose first, write on yes*.

- [x] **Reclassify its five categories** against the ADR's gate. Its worked table already settles
      four of them — an unambiguous link spelling is 🟢, the missing date keys are 🟢 announced in the
      batch, an orphan is 🟡, a `[[people/…]]` target stays 🔴. _(2026-09-12 — §3 of the skill is now
      three tiers instead of "propose first, write on yes"; version 1.2.0.)_
- [x] **Rewrite what the engine prints.** `reportLines` in `scripts/lib/wiki-lint.mjs` emits
      `Dangling links`, `Orphans`, `Frontmatter issues` — headings written for whoever built the
      scanner. _(Now: "Links pointing at a note that does not exist", "Notes nothing links to",
      "Pages your newer notes have moved past", "Notes missing their filing details"; and the two
      summary lines are "Your notes are in good shape" / "A few of your notes could use a hand".)_
- [x] 🧰 **A guard that went quiet on a rename is itself a defect, and it was found here.** The
      identity-discipline test that forbids `/lint` from inventing a person located its section by the
      heading *"3. Propose fixes"* — which this step retitled, so the test read an **empty** section
      and its two assertions had nothing to judge. Re-anchored on the step **number**, which is the
      stable part of a procedure, and the skill's own sentence reordered so the ban precedes the thing
      it bans rather than matching by accident.
- [x] ✅ **Copy the pattern that is already in that function**, rather than inventing one. The
      `unreadableNotes` section was rewritten in exactly the register this release wants, with the
      reason in a comment beside it: *"worded by what it costs rather than by what is wrong with the
      file: 'invalid YAML' means nothing to the person whose answers went stale."* The skill file
      carries the matching sentence — *"this note still answers, but with what it said in August"*.
      **One category out of five is already right. The work is to make the other four match it.**
- [x] **Keep the exit code exactly as it is.** `0` clean / `1` findings is a contract other scripts
      compose on; this release changes words and tiers, never the machine-readable half.

## Step 5 — `/consolidate`, gesture by gesture

Same treatment, and it is the second half of what the owner met in one session. Its gestures are
mostly 🔴 **and correctly so** — creating a page shapes the taxonomy, a contradiction is never
adjudicated by the brain — so the win here is **wording and batching**, not silence.

- [x] **Reclassify**, expecting most rows to stay 🔴. *A 🔴 is not a failure of the doctrine* (ADR
      0043): the goal was never fewer questions, it was that every question deserves to be there.
      _(2026-09-13 — the register's six rows held: **five stay exactly where they were**, and the
      skill now says so out loud in its Principle instead of leaving it to be re-derived.)_
- [x] **One batched proposal instead of a subset-picker per candidate** — the measured complaint was
      *about five prompts in a row*, and that is a presentation defect before it is a tier defect.
      _(Step 4 of the procedure retitled and rewritten: count first, then each candidate, and one
      turn carrying several answers — "do not come back candidate by candidate to re-confirm what
      they already answered".)_
- [x] **Every 🔴 states why it matters in one line before the options**, per ADR 0043 §5. _(With the
      contrast written into the skill: not "confirm the consolidation of these candidates" but "this
      creates the page your notes will use for this person from now on".)_
- [x] 🧰 **And the doctrine now has a guard in the two skills, not only in the constitution**
      _(`autonomy-discipline.test.mjs`)_. The existing guards pinned that the tiers **exist**; these
      pin that `/lint` and `/consolidate` **apply** them — one message rather than one per finding, the
      stake stated before the options, and the pointer to ADR 0043 rather than a second paraphrase of
      it. Anchored on the **step number**, for the reason step 4 learned the hard way. It caught one
      thing on the way in: `/lint`'s "not one per finding" lived only in its Guardrails, three
      screens below the step that needed it.

## Step 6 — the words the engine PRINTS, and the one guard that can judge them

**ADR 0043 is explicit that no deterministic guard can judge whether a sentence is plain**, and this
step does not pretend otherwise. But one honest subset *is* mechanically decidable: **a banned word in
a string the engine prints verbatim**. That is a literal in code, not a judgement about prose.

- [x] **Sweep the printed strings** — the `reportLines` class — against the register. _(2026-09-13 —
      the consolidation report was the one left: `✗ Consolidation candidates found`, `New pages to
      create`, `Entity pages to refresh`, `cited by 2`, `1 fresher`. And the staleness heading is now
      **the same sentence** the health report uses for the same fact: two names for one thing is two
      things, to whoever reads them.)_
- [x] **A vocabulary guard, test-first**, over user-facing printed literals only, with the ADR's own
      list as the seed (`orphan`, `frontmatter`, `fan-out`, `dangling`). Fail-first, then the sweep
      makes it green. _(`scripts/lib/printed-words.test.mjs` — it RUNS each report producer over a
      fixture with every section populated, so a section added later is covered the day it is
      written. `entity` joined the word list on the way, with its own plural pattern: a list whose
      entry silently misses "entities" has a hole exactly where the heading used it.)_
- [x] 🛑 **Write its blindness into the guard itself**, the way `locale-drift.mjs` states its two
      limitations in its own header: it sees **printed literals only**, never a sentence the model
      composes, and never whether what replaced the banned word is any clearer. A guard whose limits
      are undocumented gets trusted for what it does not do.
- [x] 🧰 **And the sweep paid for itself by being WRONG once, which is the test that matters**
      (CONVENTIONS §5quater). It read `  [[people/ada-lovelace]] — mentioned in 2` as a routing tag,
      because a line opening on a bracket is how the engine addresses its own dispatcher. But a
      `[[link]]` is the **owner's own notation** — the thing they type in their notes. A doubled
      bracket is now exempt, with a test in both directions so the carve-out cannot empty the rule.
- [x] **The instructions to the model are OUT of scope**, per the perimeter note above. If the sweep
      starts editing directives, it has drifted. _(Verified by re-scanning every printing line in
      `scripts/**`: the only remaining hits are a variable **named** `payload` and one named
      `dangling`. Identifiers are not prose, and nothing else printed carries a banned word.)_

## 📜 History

### Opened 2026-09-12, and what it inherited rather than decided

The parent plan had already done the hard part: the split that put the **decision** in v5.2 (shipped
as ADR 0043) and left the **audit** here. So this plan opens with its doctrine already accepted,
which is why it has no ADR step of its own — the shape v5.3 had, and the reason it could move fast.

**What the opening session measured rather than assumed**, and it changed the plan twice:

- **The jargon count is an upper bound, not a backlog.** 32 occurrences of `frontmatter` in delivered
  prose, and most of them are instructions to the model, which needs the precise word. A first draft
  of this plan treated the count as work; it would have made the brain vaguer.
- **The startup leak is narrower than the July capture and still real.** Re-reading the three emitters
  on `main` found short payloads that still carry `[wiki-health]`, `[universe]`, `[onboarding]` and
  the directive verb *offer* — so step 2 is grounded in what is there today, not in a six-week-old
  screenshot.
- **One of `/lint`'s five categories is already written the right way**, with its reason in a comment.
  That is the pattern step 4 copies, and it is worth more than any style rule this plan could state.
