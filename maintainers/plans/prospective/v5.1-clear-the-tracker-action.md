## 🎙️ THE ONE QUESTION — what "presque toutes" leaves out

Nine issues are open. **Eight are defects the fleet lives with today, and one is a feature with an
unsolved design problem.** That is the natural seam, and it is the recommendation:

- **In v5.1**: groups A, B and C — eight issues, every one of them something a user currently
  experiences as the brain being wrong.
- **Out**: **#62**, the `/feedback` path that carries an engine-level friction upstream. Its hard part
  is not the plumbing: **a friction is born inside a private vault and would travel to a public
  repository**, and the raw friction that produced #61 named a client, three colleagues and a slice of
  their business. That needs a design answer from him before any code, and holding v5.1 for it would
  hold eight fixes behind one unanswered question.

**What each answer costs.** Leaving #62 out costs nothing that is breaking today, and it keeps v5.1 a
short release. Putting it in makes v5.1 open-ended: nobody can size a de-identification design before
it exists.
